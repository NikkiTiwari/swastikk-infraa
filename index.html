Company overview

Four offices across three states, so our engineers are never far from your site.

Swastikk Infraa is an engineering contractor with offices in Jaisalmer, Jaipur, Varanasi and Patna. We take on electrical, HVAC, fire safety and civil projects for government departments, defence and institutional clients, private developers and individual customers, and participate in GeM and e-tenders.

Our work is led by qualified engineers on site, so decisions are made quickly and the quality of the job is checked at every stage. After handover, our O&M and AMC teams keep electrical and HVAC systems running.

How a project runs

One point of contact from the first site visit to the final handover.

  1. Site survey

    We visit the site, take measurements and understand the load and approvals needed.

  2. Estimate

    You get a clear, itemised quote with timelines before any work begins.

  3. Execution

    Our team builds to approved drawings and safety standards, with regular updates.

  4. Testing & commissioning

    Every installation is tested and commissioned to standard before we hand it over to you.

  5. Operation & maintenance

    After handover, our O&M and AMC teams keep your systems running with preventive and breakdown maintenance.
